# Airbnb Pricing, Market Share & Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is a Power BI dashboard that analyzes Airbnb market performance across 10 major global cities. The aim of the project was to explore how Airbnb listings differ by city, room type, pricing, customer reviews, host verification, and rating performance.

The dashboard gives a clear view of Airbnb’s market concentration, customer review behavior, seasonal demand patterns, pricing differences, and city-level performance. It is designed to help users understand which cities dominate the Airbnb market, which room types are priced higher, and how customer ratings vary across locations.

## 📊 Dataset

The analysis was built using Airbnb listings and reviews data.

The dataset includes:

- 279,712 listings
- 10 cities
- 182,024 hosts
- 144 property types
- 5.37M customer reviews

The main datasets used were:

- Listings.csv
- Reviews.csv
- Listings_data_dictionary.csv
- Reviews_data_dictionary.csv

## 🔍 Key Findings

1. **Paris is the largest Airbnb market**  
   Paris has the highest number of listings and reviews, with 64,690 listings and more than 1.2M reviews.

2. **Listings are concentrated in a few major cities**  
   Paris, New York, and Sydney account for about 48.4% of all listings, showing that Airbnb supply is heavily concentrated in key global cities.

3. **Review activity is highest in major tourist cities**  
   Paris, Rome, and New York have the highest review volumes, indicating strong customer activity in these markets.

4. **Room type strongly affects pricing**  
   Hotel rooms have the highest average price at around $800, followed by entire places at around $673. Private rooms are the lowest-priced major category at around $462.

5. **Mexico City and Rio de Janeiro have the strongest ratings**  
   These cities perform well across customer rating categories, while Hong Kong and Istanbul have the lowest average ratings.

6. **Cleanliness and value are the weakest rating areas**  
   Cleanliness and value scored lower than communication, location, and accuracy, making them important areas for host improvement.

7. **Most reviewers only leave one review**  
   Around 86.3% of reviewers left only one review, and around 98.6% left three reviews or fewer.

8. **Review activity shows seasonal patterns**  
   Paris and Rome show stronger review activity during the European summer months, while New York increases toward November and December.

9. **Listing growth peaked around 2015**  
   Airbnb listing growth increased strongly from 2011 to 2015, then slowed after 2016 and declined sharply after 2019.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

Shows the main KPIs:

- Total listings

- Total hosts

- Total reviews

Analyzes:

- Listings by city

- Cumulative city market share
- Average price by room type

### ⭐ Reviews and Host Trust Page

Analyzes:

- Review frequency by customer
- Seasonal review patterns

## ✅ Conclusion

This analysis shows that Airbnb activity is highly concentrated in a few major cities, especially Paris, New York, and Sydney. Paris leads the dataset in both listings and reviews, while Rome and New York also show strong customer activity.

Room type has a clear impact on pricing, with hotel rooms and entire-place listings being the highest-priced categories. Customer ratings are generally strong, but cleanliness and value are the weakest areas across cities.

The dashboard also highlights low repeat review activity, strong use of host trust signals, and a peak in Airbnb listing growth around 2015, followed by a sharp decline after 2019.

Overall, this project provides a clear business view of Airbnb market performance, pricing patterns, customer behavior, and city-level competitiveness.
